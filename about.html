About Me

My Journey into Sports Broadcasting

Hello, my name is Landon, and I am a junior at the University of Wisconsin–Oshkosh pursuing a degree in Radio, TV and Film. My interest in sports broadcasting has grown through hands-on production experience, campus involvement, and opportunities to work behind the scenes at sporting events. I enjoy learning new technical skills, solving problems, and working with a team to help bring live events to an audience. I'm continuing to build my experience as I work toward a career in sports media and live production.

My interest in sports broadcasting began during my sophomore year at Waunakee High School, when I joined Warrior Media to find new ways to stay connected to my school community. Through livestreaming a variety of athletic events, I discovered how much I enjoyed being part of the production process and helping bring games to an audience.

The experience challenged me to think on my feet, react quickly, solve problems, and communicate effectively with others. What began as an opportunity to stay involved with my school grew into a passion for sports media and an interest in pursuing broadcasting as a career.

Since then, I've looked for opportunities to expand my skills, learn from experienced professionals, and explore the different roles that contribute to a live sports production. Each experience has strengthened my interest in the field and motivated me to keep learning.

My Experience

Learning Through Experience

My experience with Warrior Media helped me discover the many responsibilities involved in live sports broadcasting. Livestreaming athletic events taught me to think quickly, communicate with others, and adapt when unexpected challenges arose. It also showed me how much teamwork goes into bringing a live sporting event to viewers.

Looking to expand my skills beyond high school, I reached out to the Madison Mallards and spent the summer of 2023 working as one of two camera operators for baseball games. This opportunity gave me valuable exposure to live sports production and helped me better understand the coordination and preparation required behind the camera.

I also had the opportunity to shadow media production specialists at Madison Forward Soccer, learning more about the production side of the sport and exploring different career paths within sports media. Through these experiences, I've continued to develop my technical skills, learn from others, and build a stronger understanding of the industry.

I also worked as the Director of Video Production and Broadcasting for the Lakeshore Chinooks, where I gained valuable hands-on experience in live sports broadcasting. This role allowed me to develop my technical skills, contribute to game-day productions, and gain a better understanding of the preparation and teamwork required to deliver a successful broadcast.

In addition to my other experiences, I volunteer and work with Titan TV at multiple events on the University of Wisconsin–Oshkosh campus. This involvement allows me to continue learning about campus productions and contribute to university events.

What's Next

Looking Ahead

As I continue to pursue a career in sports media, I'm excited to build on my experience in camera operation and live production. I enjoy being part of the team that brings sporting events to life for viewers and understanding how each role contributes to a successful broadcast.

Currently, I am continuing to build my experience at the University of Wisconsin–Oshkosh, where I have returned as a Game Day Operations Intern through the athletic department. This role gives me the opportunity to be involved in the preparation and coordination that help make sporting events successful. Alongside my involvement with Titan TV and other campus events, I continue to learn more about the teamwork, organization, and technical preparation required in live sports production. These experiences are helping me develop the skills I hope to bring to a future career in sports broadcasting.

Moving forward, I hope to gain more hands-on experience, strengthen my technical abilities, and learn from professionals in the industry. I'm especially interested in exploring the different aspects of live sports production and taking on new challenges that help me grow both personally and professionally.

My goal is to continue developing my skills, contribute to meaningful productions, and build a career that allows me to combine my passion for sports with my interest in broadcasting and media.
